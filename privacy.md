# Privacy policy

**Erlang C Staffing Planner**

*Last updated: 27 October 2026*

## Summary

This visual processes your data entirely inside Power BI, on the device where
the report is open. It collects nothing, stores nothing, and transmits nothing.

## What the visual accesses

Only the fields you map into it: interval, contact volume, weighted handle time,
and optionally scheduled agents and queue name. It reads them to perform the
staffing calculation and draw the chart.

## What it does with that data

The data is held in memory for as long as the visual is rendered, and discarded
when the report closes. It is not written to disk, to browser storage, to
cookies, or anywhere else.

## What is transmitted

Nothing. The visual makes no network requests of any kind. It declares no
web-access privileges in its capabilities, which Power BI enforces at runtime,
and the absence of external requests is verified as part of Microsoft's
certification audit.

## Analytics and telemetry

None. We receive no usage data, no diagnostics and no error reports from the
visual.

Aggregate information about listing views, purchases and license assignments is
available to us through Microsoft Partner Center. That is generated by
Microsoft Marketplace rather than by the visual, and is governed by Microsoft's
own privacy terms.

## Our websites

The statements above describe the Power BI visual. Separately, we operate two
public websites: **staffingcurve.com** and **erlangcalc.com**. These pages do
not use cookies and contain no advertising or tracking pixels.

**Page-view analytics.** Both websites use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count
visits. When a page loads, Cloudflare receives the page address, the referring
page, and basic technical details such as browser type, operating system, device
type and approximate country. Cloudflare states that this service does not use
cookies or local storage and does not track individual visitors across sites or
sessions. We see only aggregate counts. See Cloudflare's privacy policy at
cloudflare.com/privacypolicy for details.

**The calculator.** Everything you enter into the calculator at erlangcalc.com
is computed in your browser. The values you type are never sent to us or to
anyone else.

**Search.** We use Google Search Console to see how our sites appear in Google
search results. It reports on Google's own search data and does not place
anything on your device when you visit our pages.

**Hosting.** The websites are hosted on GitLab Pages, which, like any web host,
keeps ordinary server logs of requests (such as IP address and time). We do not
have access to individual visitors' identities through them.

## Licensing

Where a license applies, the visual asks Power BI which service plans are
assigned to the current user. That check happens between Power BI and Microsoft
using Microsoft's Licensing API. We receive no personal data from it - the
visual sees only whether a usable plan exists.

## Third-party components

The visual uses open-source components published by Microsoft for building
Power BI visuals. None of them transmit data externally.

## Children's data

The visual is business software and is not directed at children.
